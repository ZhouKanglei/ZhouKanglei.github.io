Postdoctoral Researcher @ Inria Rennes

Biography

Ziyi Chang is a postdoctoral researcher at Inria Rennes, focusing on multi-character, multi-agent, and crowd modelling.

Research Interests

  • Character animation and multi-character interaction
  • Human modelling, diffusion models, and adversarial robustness
  • Spatial-temporal modelling and generative models
